The registry files are generated by the build that tests them. `ui.yml` regenerates `apps/ui/public/r` before the starter smoke instead of failing on a diff, and master commits the items together with the conformance records — a generated file is not something a person should have to keep in step by hand, and the gate was red three times on a race between a merge and the regeneration. The items the smoke installs are still the ones the sources produce, because the regeneration happens before it.
