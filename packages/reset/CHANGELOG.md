## 0.3.0

### Minor Changes

- 265d429: Baseline variants: `@cssints/reset/widely-available`, `/newly-available`, `/baseline-2015` to `/baseline-2026` and each `/with-downstream` (and `/limited-availability`, the whole reset). A variant keeps only the declarations whose property and value are in the group, and drops a rule left with none; `@cssints/reset` stays the full reset.
- 2ba4911: The base takes the border off buttons (`button` and `input` of type `button`, `submit` and `reset`) in a `:where()` rule of the `reset` layer, so a button no longer shows the bevel of the browser; text fields, text areas and selects keep their borders.
